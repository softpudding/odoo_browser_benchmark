Go to the **Point of Sale** app and open the register **`Benchmark Cash Desk`**.

1. Open the register.
2. Record a **Cash In** of **`20.00`** with reason **`Opening float`**.
3. Create one order with product **`Bench Snack Crate`**.
4. Pay the order using **`Bench Cash`**.
5. Record a **Cash Out** of **`5.00`** with reason **`Petty cash drop`**.
6. Close the session and set the counted cash to **`27.00`**.

## Rules

- Do not add more than one product
- Do not use any payment method other than `Bench Cash`
- Do not create more than one POS order
- The session must end closed
