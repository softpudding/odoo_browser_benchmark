Navigate to the **Sales** app and open the **Quotations** list.

Find the draft quotation with customer reference **`BENCH-SO-RESERVE`** for customer **`Northwind Assembly`**.

1. Open that quotation and click **Confirm**.
2. Open its **Delivery** smart button.
3. On the delivery order, click **Check Availability** so the stock is manually reserved.
4. Then click **Validate** to complete the delivery. If an immediate transfer dialog appears, confirm it.

Rules:
- Work only on the quotation with customer reference `BENCH-SO-RESERVE`.
- Do not modify the distractor quotation `BENCH-SO-RESERVE-DIST`.
- Do not change ordered quantities, products, customer, or prices.
- The delivery must finish fully done with no backorder left open.
