Navigate to the **Sales** app and open the **Quotations** list.

Find the draft quotation for customer **Bench SO Customer** (there is only
one draft quotation for this customer, containing **5 units** of
**Bench Stockable Product**).

1. Open the quotation and click **Confirm** to convert it into a Sales
   Order.
2. Click the **Delivery** smart button at the top of the Sales Order to
   navigate to the generated delivery transfer.
3. On the delivery transfer, click **Check Availability** to manually
   reserve the stock for this transfer.
4. After the stock is reserved, click **Validate** to confirm the
   delivery.
5. If a backorder dialog appears (it should not, since all 5 units are
   in stock), dismiss it — the delivery must be fully validated with
   all 5 units delivered.

**Rules:**
- You MUST click **Check Availability** before validating. Do NOT skip
  straight to Validate.
- All 5 units must be delivered — do NOT create a backorder.
- Do NOT modify the product, customer, price, or quantity on the order.
- Do NOT cancel the quotation or the delivery.
