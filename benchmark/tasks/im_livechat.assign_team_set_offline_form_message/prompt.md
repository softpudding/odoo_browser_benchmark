Go to the **Live Chat** app and open the existing channel named `Website Support`.

The Live Chat kanban opens a chat session by default — to reach the channel form, hover the `Website Support` kanban card, click the 3-dots menu in the corner, and choose **Configure Channel**.

On the channel form, switch to the **Options** tab and set these two text fields to the exact values below:

- **Notification Text** (the Live Chat button text): `Leave us a message and we will follow up by Monday.`
- **Welcome Message** (the message shown when a visitor opens the chat window): `Hello — we are offline. Email support@bench.test and we will reply by Monday.`

Save the channel.

**Rules:**
- Do not rename the `Website Support` channel.
- Do not change any colors, session limits, or the review link.
- Do not modify or delete any other live chat channel.
- Do not add or remove operators on the channel.
- Do not add any rules, chatbots, or canned responses.
