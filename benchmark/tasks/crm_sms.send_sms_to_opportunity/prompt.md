Go to the **CRM** app.

Open the opportunity **"Bench Hot Lead"** (from the pipeline kanban).

Send an SMS to the contact from this opportunity:

1. On the opportunity form, open the cog **Actions** menu (the settings/gear icon in the control panel next to the breadcrumb).
2. Click **Send SMS**.
3. In the dialog that opens, confirm the recipient number is **+1 617-555-1234** (leave it as-is).
4. In the **Message** field, type exactly:

   `Following up on your inquiry. - Bench team`

5. Click the **Send** button in the dialog footer.

**Rules:**
- Do not change the opportunity's stage, partner, or any other field.
- Do not change the recipient phone number.
- Do not close the dialog without sending.
- Do not send more than one SMS.
