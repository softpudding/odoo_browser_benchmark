Agent must: open the receipt, click the line's **Details** button (opens the subcontracting move form dialog), ensure two move_line rows, enter `BSA-2026-01` / `BSA-2026-02` (qty 1 each) via autocomplete Create, save the popup, then Validate the receipt header. On validate, the subcontract MO splits into two lot-specific MOs (one per serial), both become done, and the raw components are back-flushed from the subcontracting location (-2 Part-A, -2 Part-B). |
