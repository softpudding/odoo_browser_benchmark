Transfer the vehicle `Bench Sedan BS-1001` from its current driver to a new driver and make sure the assignation history is updated.

1. Open the Fleet app.
2. Find and open the vehicle with license plate `BS-1001` (named `Bench Sedan BS-1001`).
3. The Driver field currently shows `Driver Alice`. Change the Driver to `Driver Bob`.
4. Save the vehicle.
5. Open the `Drivers History` smart button on the vehicle form. Verify that there are at least two entries: one for `Driver Alice` and one for `Driver Bob`.
6. Edit the assignation log entry for `Driver Bob` so that its Start Date is `04/18/2026`. Save.

Rules:
- Keep the vehicle's final Driver as `Driver Bob`.
- Do not delete the existing assignation log entry for `Driver Alice`.
- The assignation log entry for `Driver Bob` must have Start Date = `2026-04-18`.
- Do not create more than one new vehicle, partner, or assignation log beyond what the task requires.
