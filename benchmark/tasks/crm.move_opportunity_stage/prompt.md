Go to the **CRM** app.

Find the opportunity named **"Acme Corporation"** in the pipeline.

**Move this opportunity to the "Qualified" stage.** You can do this by
dragging the opportunity card to the "Qualified" column in the kanban view,
or by opening the opportunity form and selecting "Qualified" from the stage
selector (the pill-shaped button near the top of the form showing the
current stage name).

After moving the opportunity, make sure to **save** if the form view does
not auto-save.

**Rules:**
- Do not change the opportunity's expected revenue
- Do not modify any other opportunities
- Do not delete or archive the opportunity
