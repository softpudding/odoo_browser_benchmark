Go to the **eCommerce Products** page.

Open the product **`Docking Console`** and configure **`Protective Sleeve`** as its optional product.

Make sure **`Protective Sleeve`** is published to the website.

Then open the website shop for **`Docking Console`**, add it to the cart, and accept the optional upsell so that **both products** end up in the same cart.

## Rules

- Do not rename either product
- Do not create variants
- Do not add any product other than `Docking Console` and `Protective Sleeve`
