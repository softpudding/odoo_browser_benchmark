Go to the **Point of Sale** app and open the register **`Bench Discount POS`** (accept the default opening cash of 0 if asked).

1. Add **1** unit of **`Bench POS Pen`** to the cart (unit price is €10.00).
2. Add **1** unit of **`Bench POS Notebook`** to the cart (unit price is €20.00).
3. Select the **`Bench POS Notebook`** line and apply a **10% discount** on that line **only** (use the on-screen **`% Disc`** / **Discount** numpad button, enter **10**). The Pen line must remain at €10.00 with no discount. After the discount, the Notebook line subtotal should be **€18.00** and the cart total should be **€28.00**.
4. Click **Payment**, choose the **`Bench Discount Cash`** payment method, then validate the payment so the receipt screen appears. The final order total must be **€28.00**.

## Rules

- Do not add any other product to the cart
- Do not change the quantity or unit price of either product
- Do not apply a discount on the `Bench POS Pen` line
- Do not apply a global / whole-order discount — the 10% must be on the Notebook line only
- Do not close the POS session after the sale
