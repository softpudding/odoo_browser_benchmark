Go to the **Surveys** app (URL `/odoo/surveys`) and click **New** to create a survey from scratch.

## Header

- **Survey Title**: `Bench Customer Feedback`
- Keep this as a regular feedback survey — do **not** turn it into an Assessment / Certification.

## Options tab

- **Pagination**: `One page with all the questions`
- **Access Mode**: `Anyone with the link` (so the share link is usable)
- The survey must remain **active** (not archived).

## Questions tab

Create exactly **one section / page** named `Feedback`, containing **3 questions** in this order:

1. **Question**: `How satisfied are you?`
   - Type: `Multiple choice: only one answer`
   - Answers (in this order): `Very satisfied`, `Satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Unsatisfied`

2. **Question**: `What can we improve?`
   - Type: `Multiple Lines Text Box` (free text)

3. **Question**: `Would you recommend us?`
   - Type: `Multiple choice: only one answer`
   - Answers (in this order): `Yes`, `No`, `Maybe`

## Save & share

- **Save** the survey.
- Confirm the **Access Mode** on the Options tab is `Anyone with the link` so the Share link works.

## Rules

- Do **NOT** create a second survey or leave behind drafts with another title.
- Do **NOT** add extra sections, pages, or questions.
- Do **NOT** mark this as a Certification.
- Do **NOT** archive the survey.
- The survey title must be EXACTLY `Bench Customer Feedback` (no trailing whitespace, no suffix).
- Each multiple-choice question must have the answer list EXACTLY as listed above (no extra or empty options).
