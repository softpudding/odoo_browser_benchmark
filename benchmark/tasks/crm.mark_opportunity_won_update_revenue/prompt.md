# Mark opportunity won and update expected revenue

You are on the Odoo 19 CRM app pipeline. The sales rep has just closed a deal
and needs the pipeline updated with the final revenue.

## What to do

1. In the CRM pipeline, locate the opportunity
   **"Crestline Networks - Managed VPN"**.
2. Open it.
3. On the opportunity form, set the **Expected Revenue** field to
   **52000** (the final contract value).
4. Set **Priority** to **Medium** (**2 stars**).
5. In the status bar (the breadcrumb-like row of stages / buttons at the top
   of the form), click the **Won** button. The opportunity's stage turns to a
   **Won** stage.
6. Save if anything is still unsaved (the Won action saves automatically).

## Rules

- Two other opportunities in the pipeline are named
  **"Crestline Networks - Firewall Expansion"** and
  **"Crestline Retail - POS Refresh"** — do **not** change them in any way.
  They must stay in their original stages with their original expected
  revenue.
- Do **not** mark the opportunity as Lost; use **Won** only.
- Do **not** change the customer, salesperson, or tags on any opportunity.
- Do **not** delete or archive any record.
