# Sell an event ticket and fill the attendee details

You are in Odoo 19 logged in as **Administrator**. The events team has
set up the conference **Bench Festival 2026** (June 15, 2026) with a
single ticket type **Standard** linked to the service product
**Bench Standard Ticket**. The customer **Bench Attendee Customer** has
already been created in Contacts.

You need to:

1. Sell one **Standard** ticket to **Bench Attendee Customer** as a
   confirmed sales order,
2. Fill in the attendee details on the registration that Odoo
   automatically generates from the confirmed order.

## Step 1 — Create a sales order for one ticket

1. Open the **Sales** app.
2. Click **New** to create a new quotation.
3. Set the **Customer** to `Bench Attendee Customer`.
4. In the **Order Lines** tab, click **Add a product** and pick
   `Bench Standard Ticket`.
5. If an **Configure an event** dialog opens, set:
   - **Event**: `Bench Festival 2026`
   - **Event Ticket**: `Standard`

   Then click **Ok** to close the configurator.
6. Make sure the line **Quantity** is `1`.
7. Click **Confirm** to confirm the sales order.

## Step 2 — Fill in the attendee details

Confirming the sales order automatically creates an
**event registration** for the Standard ticket on
**Bench Festival 2026**. You must now fill the attendee details on
that registration.

- If the **Event Registrations** attendee-details dialog pops up when
  you confirm the SO, fill the row directly:
  - **Name**: `Bench Attendee Name`
  - **Email**: `bench.attendee@example.com`

  Then click **Create/Update registrations** to close the dialog.

- If no dialog appears (or it was dismissed), open the **Events** app,
  open **Bench Festival 2026**, click the **Attendees** stat button,
  open the auto-generated registration linked to the Standard ticket,
  and edit:
  - **Attendee Name**: `Bench Attendee Name`
  - **Email**: `bench.attendee@example.com`

  Save the registration.

## Final state

- One **sale.order** for **Bench Attendee Customer** in **Sales Order**
  state (`sale`), with exactly one order line for `Bench Standard
  Ticket`, quantity `1`, linked to the **Standard** ticket on
  **Bench Festival 2026**.
- One **event.registration** on **Bench Festival 2026** linked to the
  **Standard** ticket, with **Attendee Name**
  `Bench Attendee Name` and **Email** `bench.attendee@example.com`.
  Its backend state is **Registered** (`open`).

## Rules

- Use the seeded **Bench Standard Ticket** product and the seeded
  **Standard** ticket on **Bench Festival 2026**. Do NOT create a new
  product and do NOT create a new ticket type on the event.
- Create exactly **one** sales order and exactly **one**
  registration (the one generated by confirming the order).
- The attendee **Name** must be exactly `Bench Attendee Name` and the
  **Email** must be exactly `bench.attendee@example.com`.
- Do **NOT** cancel, attend (check in) or otherwise close the
  registration — it must stay in the **Registered** (`open`) state.
- Do **NOT** modify the event name, dates, or the Standard ticket
  definition.
