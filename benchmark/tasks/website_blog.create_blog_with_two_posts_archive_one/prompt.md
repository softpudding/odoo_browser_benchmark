Create a blog with two published posts, then archive one of them.

1. Open the Blog app (Website > Site > Blogs, or navigate to the back-office Blogs list).
2. Create a new blog named `Bench Insights`.
3. Inside that blog, create a new blog post titled `April Update` and publish it.
4. Inside the same blog, create a second blog post titled `March Notes` and publish it.
5. After both posts exist and are published, open the post `March Notes` and archive it (use the form's Action > Archive menu so that the post's Active flag becomes off). Do NOT archive `April Update`.

Rules:
- Both posts must end up belonging to the blog `Bench Insights`.
- The post `April Update` must remain Active and Published when you finish.
- The post `March Notes` must end up Archived (Active = off) and unpublished. Its record must NOT be deleted — only archived.
- Do not create any other blogs or posts.
- Do not change the titles of the posts after you create them.
