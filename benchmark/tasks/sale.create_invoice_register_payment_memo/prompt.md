Go to the **Sales** app.

Find the confirmed sales order **`PAYFLOW-01`** for **`Brightwave Labs`**.

Complete the workflow as follows:

1. Click **Create Invoice**.
2. In the dialog, keep the default draft invoice option and create the invoice.
3. On the generated invoice, click **Confirm**.
4. Click **Pay**.
5. In the **Register Payment** dialog, set:
   - **Payment Date** to **05/21/2026**
   - **Memo** to **`PAYFLOW-MAY-SETTLEMENT`**
6. Click **Create Payment**.

**Rules:**
- Do not edit the sales order lines or invoice lines
- Do not create more than one invoice
- Do not register a payment for any other invoice
- Do not cancel the sales order
