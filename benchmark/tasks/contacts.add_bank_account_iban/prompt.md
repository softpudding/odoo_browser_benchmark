# Add a bank account to a company contact

You are in the Odoo 19 **Contacts** app logged in as **Administrator**.

## What to do

1. Open the **Contacts** app.
2. Find and open the company **Ridgeway Commercial Services**.
3. Click the **Invoicing** tab on the form.
4. In the **Bank accounts** field at the top of the tab, add a new
   bank account with the IBAN:

   `DE89370400440532013000`

   A create dialog may open when you enter the number — fill in the
   **Account Number** field with the IBAN above and save the dialog.
5. **Save** the contact form.

After saving, the partner **Ridgeway Commercial Services** should have
exactly one bank account whose account number is
`DE89370400440532013000`.

## Rules

- Use the exact account number above — do **not** invent additional
  digits, formats, or spaces.
- Do **not** create bank accounts on any other partner.
- Do **not** delete, archive, or rename **Ridgeway Commercial
  Services**.
- Do **not** modify any existing bank accounts on other partners.
