Go to the **Point of Sale** app and open the register **`Bench POS`** (confirm the opening cash of **0** if prompted).

1. Click on **`Bench POS Item A`** in the product grid to add one unit to the cart (€20.00).
2. Click on **`Bench POS Item B`** in the product grid to add one unit to the cart (€15.00). The cart subtotal must now be **€35.00**.
3. Apply the pre-agreed fixed **€5.00** global discount using the on-screen **`Discount`** control button (the one with the tag icon, sometimes labelled `Disc`). If the button is not visible on the product screen, open the **Actions / ellipsis menu** in the top-right of the product screen and click `Discount` from there. When the percentage popup opens it is **pre-filled with `14.29`** — **accept this default value** and confirm. A new discount line with a price of **-5.00 EUR** must appear on the order, and the order total must drop to **€30.00**.
4. Click **Payment**, choose **`Bench Cash`** as the payment method, type the exact tendered amount **`30`** on the on-screen keypad, then click **Validate** so the receipt screen appears.

## Rules

- Do not add any other product to the cart
- Do not change the unit price of either product
- Do not invoice the order
- Do not close the POS session after the sale
- Use the global **Discount** control button — do **not** apply a per-line discount via the numpad "Disc" mode
- Keep the pre-filled **14.29%** value in the Discount popup; do not change it
