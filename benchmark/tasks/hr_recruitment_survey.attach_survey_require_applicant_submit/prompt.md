Go to the **Recruitment** app.

Find the job position **"Bench Recruit Lead"** and open its **form view**
(not the applicant kanban). The fastest path is to switch the Job
Positions view to the list view via the breadcrumb, then click the
row for "Bench Recruit Lead".

On the job form:

1. Open the **Recruitment** tab (labelled "Details" in some views — it
   is the tab that contains the Hiring Process / Job Posting groups,
   NOT the Summary tab).
2. In that tab, find the **Interview Form** field and pick the existing
   survey **"Bench Screening"** from the autocomplete dropdown. Do NOT
   create a new survey — the dropdown must show "Bench Screening" as an
   existing option. If you do not see it, keep typing `Bench` until it
   appears.
3. Save the job.

Then navigate to the existing applicant **"Alice Candidate"** on this
job (open the job's applicant kanban via the smart button or the
Recruitment > Applicants menu) and open her applicant form.

4. Verify that the header **"Send Interview"** button is now visible on
   the applicant form. You do not need to click it — just confirming
   it renders is enough. This button only appears once a survey is
   attached to the job.

**Rules:**
- Do NOT create a new survey or click "Interview Form" in any
  Configure-like flow that creates a blank survey.
- Do NOT change the applicant's stage, name, email, phone, or any
  other field.
- Do NOT archive or delete the job or the applicant.
