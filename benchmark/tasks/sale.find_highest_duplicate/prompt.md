Go to the Sales app and look at ALL quotations (remove the "My Quotations" filter if active).

There are several draft quotations for different customers. Some customers also have **confirmed** Sales Orders (status = "Sales Order").

Your job: find the customer who has the **highest total value of confirmed Sales Orders** (not draft quotations). Then, among that customer's **draft** quotations only, find and **Duplicate** the one with the **smallest** untaxed amount.

Use the Actions menu (⋮ or gear icon → Duplicate) to duplicate it.

Steps you need to take:
1. Look at all confirmed Sales Orders, group or sum them by customer
2. Identify which customer has the highest confirmed total
3. Switch to looking at only that customer's draft quotations
4. Duplicate the one with the smallest amount

Do NOT confirm any quotation. Leave everything as draft.
