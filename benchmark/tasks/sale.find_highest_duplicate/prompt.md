Go to the Sales app and look at the list of quotations for customer "Azure Interior".

There are several quotations with different untaxed amounts. Find the quotation with the **highest untaxed amount** among all draft quotations for Azure Interior.

Once you have identified it, open that quotation and use the Actions menu (gear icon) to **Duplicate** it.

Do NOT confirm either the original or the duplicate. Leave both as draft quotations.
