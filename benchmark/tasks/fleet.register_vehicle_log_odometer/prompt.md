Register a new vehicle in Fleet and log an odometer reading for it.

1. Open the **Fleet** app.
2. Click **New** to create a new vehicle with the following details:
   - **Model**: `Model 3` (select the existing model under the `Tesla Motors` brand — do not create a new model)
   - **License Plate**: `BENCH-001`
3. **Save** the vehicle.
4. From the Fleet menu, open **Vehicles > Odometer** (the Odometers list).
5. Click **New** and log a reading for the vehicle you just created:
   - **Vehicle**: `Tesla Motors/Model 3/BENCH-001` (the vehicle you just created)
   - **Odometer Value**: `1250`
6. **Save** the odometer log.

**Rules:**
- Create exactly one new vehicle with license plate `BENCH-001`.
- Do not modify any existing vehicle.
- The odometer reading must be exactly `1250` (in kilometers, the default unit).
- Do not create any extra odometer logs.
