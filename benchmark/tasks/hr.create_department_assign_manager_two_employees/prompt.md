# Set Up the Bench Ops Department

You need to create a new department and move two workers into it.

## Step 1 - Create the department

1. Go to the **Employees** app, then open the **Departments** view (left sidebar **Departments** menu).
2. Click **New** to create a new department.
3. Set the department **Name** to `Bench Ops`.
4. Set the **Manager** to `Bench Dept Manager`.
5. **Save** the department record.

## Step 2 - Reassign the two workers

Open the **Employees** app's main employee list and, for each of the
following employees in turn, open their form, change the **Department**
field to `Bench Ops`, and **Save**:

1. `Bench Dept Worker 1`
2. `Bench Dept Worker 2`

## Rules

- The department name must be exactly `Bench Ops`.
- The department manager must be `Bench Dept Manager`.
- Both workers (`Bench Dept Worker 1` and `Bench Dept Worker 2`) must
  end up in the `Bench Ops` department.
- Do not change any other fields on the workers (job title, work
  email, phone, etc.).
- Do not archive or delete any employees or departments.
- Do not set `Bench Dept Manager`'s own department to `Bench Ops`
  (the manager stays without a department).
