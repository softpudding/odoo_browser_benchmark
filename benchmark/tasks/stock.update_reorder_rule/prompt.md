Go to the **Inventory** app.

Navigate to **Operations → Replenishment**. Click on the **Reorder Rules** tab (if not already visible, look for "Reorder Rules" in the Replenishment view).

Find the reorder rule for product **"Steel Bracket 12\""** at location **WH/Stock**.

Update the rule:

1. Change the **Min Quantity** from its current value to **50.00**.
2. Change the **Max Quantity** from its current value to **200.00**.

Save the changes.

**Rules:**
- Only modify the reorder rule for "Steel Bracket 12\""
- Do not change any other reorder rules
- Do not trigger replenishment or create purchase orders
- Do not modify the product itself
