Go to the **Point of Sale** app and open the register **`Bench Easy POS`** (accept the default opening cash of 0 if asked).

1. Add **1** unit of **`Bench Easy POS Item`** to the cart (unit price is €15.00).
2. Click the **Customer** button on the action pad and select the existing customer **`Bench POS Easy Customer`**. The customer name must appear on the order header.
3. Click **Payment**, choose the **`Bench Easy Cash`** payment method, then validate the payment so the receipt screen appears. The final order total must be **€15.00**.

## Rules

- Do not add any other product to the cart
- Do not change the quantity or unit price of `Bench Easy POS Item`
- Do not close the POS session after the sale
- Use the existing `Bench POS Easy Customer` — do NOT create a new customer
