# Tag three existing blog posts with a new Q2-2026 tag

Create a brand-new blog tag and add it to three specific existing posts.

## Step 1 - Create the new tag

1. In the Website back-office, open **Site > Blog Tags** (or navigate to the Blog Tags back-office list at `/odoo/blog-tags`).
2. Click **New** to create a new tag.
3. Set the tag **Name** to `Q2-2026`.
4. **Save** the tag record.

## Step 2 - Assign the tag to three posts (one at a time)

Go back to the Blog Posts back-office list (Website > Site > Blog Posts, or `/odoo/blog-post-pages`). For **each** of the following three posts in turn, open its form, add the `Q2-2026` tag to its **Tags** field (pick the existing tag from the autocomplete dropdown — do NOT create a new one), and **Save** the form:

1. `Post-A`
2. `Post-B`
3. `Post-C`

## Rules

- The tag name must be exactly `Q2-2026` (capital Q, hyphen, four digits).
- Create the tag ONCE. Do not create duplicates.
- All three posts `Post-A`, `Post-B`, `Post-C` must have the `Q2-2026` tag on their `Tags` field when you finish.
- The fourth seeded post `Post-D` must **NOT** receive the `Q2-2026` tag.
- Do not change any other field on any post (title, blog, subtitle, content, published state, author, etc.).
- Do not delete, archive, or create any blog posts.
- Do not publish or unpublish any posts.
