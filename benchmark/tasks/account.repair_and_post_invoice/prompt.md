Open the Invoicing (Accounting) app. There are two draft customer invoices for **Beacon Design**.

Edit **only** the invoice whose source document is `APR-OPS`.
In this UI, that field may appear as **Invoice Origin** / **Source Document** in the invoice list or under **Other Info**.

For that invoice:

1. From **Invoicing > Customers > Products**, open each product from the invoice and read its **Sales Description** field on the product form.
2. The Sales Description gives the correct invoice **Quantity** and **Discount** for that product in the format `Qty=X | Discount=Y`.
3. Return to the draft invoice and fix every product line so the quantity and discount match the instruction.
4. Open **Other Info** and set **Customer Reference** to `APR-OPS-READY`.
5. Post the invoice.

Do not modify the other draft invoice.
