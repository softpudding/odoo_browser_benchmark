Open the Calendar app. There is a **weekly recurring event** called **"Ops Standup"** that occurs every Monday for 4 weeks, starting 2026-04-13.

The four occurrences are:
- Mon Apr 13 at 09:00 — location: "Main Office"
- Mon Apr 20 at 09:00 — location: "Main Office"
- Mon Apr 27 at 09:00 — location: "Main Office"
- Mon May 04 at 09:00 — location: "Main Office"

Your job is to change **only the April 27 occurrence** (the third one):

1. Navigate to the week of April 27 in the calendar.
2. Click on the **"Ops Standup"** event on **Monday April 27**.
3. Edit this event and change its **Location** from "Main Office" to **"Building B Room 4"**.
4. When saving, you will be asked whether to apply the change to **This Event**, **This and Following Events**, or **All Events**. Choose **"This Event"** only.

After completing this, the April 27 occurrence should have location "Building B Room 4", while all other occurrences (Apr 13, Apr 20, May 4) must still have location "Main Office".

**Rules:**
- Only the April 27 occurrence's location should change
- Do not modify any other occurrence
- Do not delete or cancel any events
- Do not change the event time, name, or any other field
