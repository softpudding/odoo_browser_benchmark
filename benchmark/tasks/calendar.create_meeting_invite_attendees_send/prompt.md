1. **Title**: `Team Sync`
2. **Date**: May 20, 2026
3. **Start time**: 10:00 AM (10:00)
4. **End time**: 11:00 AM (11:00)
5. **Attendees**: add the two seeded contacts **Attendee Alpha** and **Attendee Beta**
   from the Attendees autocomplete (pick the existing contacts — do NOT create

- The event title must be exactly `Team Sync`.
- The event must be a **one-time** event (no recurrence) and **not all-day**,
  timed from 10:00 to 11:00 local time on 2026-05-20.
- Attendees must include exactly the two existing contacts Attendee Alpha and
  Attendee Beta (do not create new contacts, do not add anyone else beyond the
  organizer who is added automatically).
- Do NOT set a location, description, reminder, or videocall link.
- Do NOT schedule any activities in the chatter.
- Do NOT modify any other existing events, contacts, or alarms.
