# Partially Pay The Target Vendor Bill

Open the posted vendor bill `VB-PARTIAL-01` for **Summit Office Supply** and register a partial payment.

1. Go to **Vendor Bills**.
2. Open bill `VB-PARTIAL-01`.
3. Click **Register Payment**.
4. In the payment dialog:
   - set **Amount** to `600.00`
   - set **Payment Date** to `June 12, 2026`
   - set **Memo** to `PARTIAL-JUN-600`
5. Click **Create Payment**.

## Rules

- Only pay bill `VB-PARTIAL-01`.
- Do not fully pay the bill.
- Do not edit or pay bill `VB-PARTIAL-02`.
