Schedule a follow-up activity on a fleet vehicle via the chatter.

1. Open the **Fleet** app.
2. In **Vehicles**, open the vehicle whose model is **Benchmark Utility Truck** (license plate `BENCH-ACT-001`).
3. In the chatter at the bottom of the form, click **Activities** (or **Schedule Activity**).
4. In the activity dialog that appears, fill these fields:
   - **Activity Type**: `To-Do` (also shown as `To Do`)
   - **Due Date**: `July 20, 2026` (`07/20/2026`)
   - **Summary**: `Yearly Safety Inspection`
   - Leave **Assigned to** as the default (Mitchell Admin).
5. Click **Schedule** (or **Schedule Activity**) to save the activity.

**Rules:**
- Do NOT modify the vehicle itself (model, driver, license plate).
- Do NOT create another vehicle.
- Do NOT log a service or contract record.
- Create exactly one activity linked to this vehicle.
