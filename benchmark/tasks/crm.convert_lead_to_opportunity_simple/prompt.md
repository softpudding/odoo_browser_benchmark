Go to the **CRM** app.

Open the **Leads** view (from the top menu **Leads > My Pipeline / Leads**;
if the Leads menu is not visible, go to **Configuration > Settings**,
tick **Leads**, click **Save**, then return to **CRM > Leads**).

Find the lead titled **"Spark Innovations - Pager SaaS"**.

1. Open the lead form.
2. Click **Convert to Opportunity** in the status bar (top action buttons).
3. In the wizard:
   - Leave the action as **"Convert to opportunity"** (the default).
   - Under **Customer**, choose **"Link to an existing customer"** and set
     it to **Spark Innovations**.
4. Click **Create Opportunity** to finish.
5. Move the opportunity to the **Qualified** stage.
6. In the chatter, schedule a **Call** activity with:
   - **Summary:** `Confirm rollout timeline`
   - **Due Date:** `05/15/2026`

**Rules:**
- Do NOT merge with any other lead.
- Do NOT create a brand-new customer.
- Do NOT touch any other lead or opportunity.
- Do NOT change the lead's name, description, revenue, or salesperson before
  converting.
