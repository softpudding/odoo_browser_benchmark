Go to the **Manufacturing** app and open **Manufacturing Orders**.

1. Find the manufacturing order **WH/MO/BENCH-001** in the list.
2. Open it.
3. Click the **Produce All** button in the form header to produce
   the full quantity and mark the order as done.

If a confirmation dialog or backorder wizard appears, accept the
defaults and confirm.

**Rules:**
- Do NOT change the quantity to produce.
- Do NOT modify or delete any component lines.
- Do NOT cancel the manufacturing order.
