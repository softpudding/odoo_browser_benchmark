Open the **Inventory** app and go to **Operations > Physical Inventory**.

Find the row for product **`Bench Stock Counter`** at location **`WH/Stock`**.

Update that row as follows:

1. Set **Counted Quantity** to **`25`**.
2. Set the inventory **Date** (the "Scheduled" / inventory date column on the line) to **`03/20/2026`** (March 20, 2026).
3. Click **Apply** on that row to post the inventory adjustment.

## Rules

- Only adjust the row for `Bench Stock Counter` at `WH/Stock`.
- Do not change any other product or location.
- Do not click **Apply All**; use the per-row **Apply** action.
- Do not delete the quant or change its location.
- Use the date `03/20/2026` exactly (March 20, 2026).
