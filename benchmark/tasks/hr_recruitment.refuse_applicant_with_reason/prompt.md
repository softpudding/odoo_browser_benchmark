# Refuse applicant with a specific reason

You are on the Odoo 19 Recruitment app. A hiring manager has asked you to
refuse a single applicant and record the correct reason so the pipeline stays
clean.

## What to do

1. In the left sidebar of the Recruitment app, open the job position
   **"Senior Platform Engineer"**.
2. In the kanban of applicants for that job, open the applicant named
   **"Marcus Whitfield"**.
3. On the applicant form, click the **Refuse** button (it is in the status
   bar / header area of the form).
4. A **Refuse Reason** wizard opens. In that wizard:
   - Set **Refuse Reason** to **"Does not fit the job requirements"**.
   - **Uncheck** the **Send Email** checkbox if it is checked — we do not
     want an email sent to the candidate from this action.
   - Do **not** tick "Refuse Duplicate Applications".
5. Click the **Refuse Reason** apply button at the bottom of the wizard
   (the primary button — label may be "Refuse Reason", "Refuse", or
   "Submit"). The wizard closes and the applicant is archived.

## Rules

- Do **not** refuse any other applicant. The two other candidates on the
  same job ("Ella Navarro" and "Devon Burke") must stay active.
- Do **not** change the refuse reason on any applicant other than Marcus
  Whitfield.
- Do **not** delete the applicant record — use the Refuse flow.
- Do **not** send an email; untick "Send Email" in the wizard if needed.
