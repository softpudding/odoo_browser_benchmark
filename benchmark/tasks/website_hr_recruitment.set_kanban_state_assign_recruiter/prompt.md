Open the **Recruitment** app.

Find the job position **"Senior QA Engineer"** and open it to see its applicants.

Open the applicant **"Diana Recruit"**.

Make the following changes on her applicant form:

1. Change the **Kanban State** to **"Ready for Next Stage"** (the green ball
   shown next to the applicant name at the top of the form). Click the small
   colored status ball and select **Ready for Next Stage** from the dropdown.
2. Set the **Recruiter** field to **"Recruiter Bob"** (use the autocomplete).
3. In the chatter at the bottom of the form, click **"Log note"** (NOT "Send
   message"), type exactly:

   ```
   Phone screen done.
   ```

   then click **Log** (or **Send**) to post the internal note.
4. Save the applicant.

**Rules:**
- Do not change the applicant's name, email, phone, or stage.
- Do not refuse, archive or delete the applicant.
- The note must be an **internal log note**, not a public message.
- Do not modify any other applicants.
