Go to the **Invoicing** app and open the customer invoice with reference **BENCH-PAY-01** for **Lakeview Partners**.

Complete the following steps:

1. **Register a payment** on this invoice. Click the **Pay** button in the
   top-right of the invoice form. This opens the "Register Payment" wizard
   dialog.

2. **Accept all defaults** in the wizard dialog. Do NOT change the amount,
   journal, payment date, or any other field. The amount should already
   show $1,000.00.

3. Click **Create Payment** to confirm and create the payment.

4. **Verify the payment was registered.** After clicking Create Payment,
   you should be taken to the payment form, or the invoice should show
   a "Paid" status. Confirm that the invoice payment state changed to
   **Paid** or **In Payment**.

**Rules:**
- Accept ALL default values in the payment wizard — do not modify any fields
- The payment amount must remain $1,000.00
- Do not change the payment journal, date, or memo
- Do not create additional payments
- Do not modify the invoice in any other way
