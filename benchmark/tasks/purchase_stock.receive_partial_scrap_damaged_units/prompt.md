# Receive a partial shipment and scrap damaged units, no backorder

You are in the Odoo 19 **Purchase** app logged in as **Administrator**.

Purchase order with source document **`BENCH-SCRAP-RCV-01`** is a confirmed PO
with vendor **Bench Reliable Supplier** for **10 units** of **Bench Widget**
at **€5 each**. The vendor has shipped all 10 units, but on inspection **2
of the 10 units arrived damaged** and must be **scrapped** (tagged as
`Damaged`). The supplier has confirmed they will **not** replace the damaged
units, so the remaining quantity must **not** be carried over on a backorder.

## What to do

1. Open the **Purchase** app and find purchase order with source document
   **`BENCH-SCRAP-RCV-01`** (vendor **Bench Reliable Supplier**).
2. Click the **Receive** button (or the receipt smart button) to open the
   incoming transfer generated by the PO.
3. From the receipt form, open the **Actions** (cog / gear) menu and click
   **Scrap**. A **Scrap Products** dialog opens.
4. In the Scrap Products dialog, fill in:
   - **Product:** `Bench Widget`
   - **Quantity:** `2`
   - **Scrap Reason:** add the existing tag `Damaged`
   - Leave **Source Location** and **Scrap Location** at their defaults.
5. Click **Scrap Products** in the dialog footer to validate the scrap.
   The scrap record must end up in **Done** state.
6. Back on the receipt form, update the **Done** quantity on the
   Bench Widget move line to **8** (the 8 good units that will go to stock).
7. Click **Validate** on the receipt header.
8. When the **Create Backorder?** dialog appears, click **No Backorder** so
   the 2 damaged units are **not** carried over onto a second receipt.

After you finish:

- The receipt for PO `BENCH-SCRAP-RCV-01` is in **Done** state with
  the Bench Widget move line quantity equal to **8**.
- Exactly **one** `stock.scrap` record exists for **Bench Widget**, in
  **Done** state, with quantity **2** and the **Damaged** reason tag.
- The purchase order has exactly **one** receipt (no backorder was
  generated).

## Rules

- Do **not** cancel the purchase order or the receipt.
- Do **not** change the ordered quantity (it must stay at **10**).
- Do **not** change the unit price (€5.00) or the product.
- Scrap **exactly 2** units of **Bench Widget** — do not create multiple
  scrap records, and do not scrap any other product.
- The scrap reason tag must be **Damaged** (the tag already exists —
  select it from the dropdown, do not create a new one).
- The receipt's final done quantity must be exactly **8**.
- Do **not** create a backorder (choose **No Backorder** in the wizard).
