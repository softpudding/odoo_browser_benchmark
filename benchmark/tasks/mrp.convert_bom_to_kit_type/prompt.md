Go to the **Manufacturing** app and open **Products → Bills of Materials**.

1. Find the Bill of Materials for the product **Benchmark Modular Shelving**.
2. Open it.
3. In the **BOM Type** field (the radio / selector labeled *BoM Type* on the
   form, default value *Manufacture this Product*), change it to **Kit**.
4. Save the record.

**Rules:**
- Do NOT modify the BOM components or their quantities.
- Do NOT change the product or the Quantity field.
- Do NOT create a new BOM — edit the existing one.
