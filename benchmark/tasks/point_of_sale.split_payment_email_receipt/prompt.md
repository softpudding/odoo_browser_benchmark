Go to the **Point of Sale** app and open the register **`Benchmark Bistro`**.

Create one order for customer **`Harbor Receipt Customer`** with exactly one product:

- `Harbor Granola Box`

Pay the order by splitting the total across these two payment methods:

- `Bench Cash`: `5.00`
- `Bench Card`: the remaining balance

Then email the receipt to **`harbor.receipts@example.com`**.

## Rules

- Do not add extra products
- Do not invoice the order
- Do not create more than one POS order
