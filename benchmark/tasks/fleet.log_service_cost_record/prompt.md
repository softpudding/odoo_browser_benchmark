Log a service record against an existing fleet vehicle.

1. Open the **Fleet** app.
2. In **Vehicles**, open the vehicle whose model is **Benchmark Fleet Sedan** (license plate `BENCH-001`).
3. Click the **Services** stat button in the button box at the top of the vehicle form.
4. Click **New** to create a new service record.
5. Fill these fields:
   - **Service Type**: `Oil Change` (create it on the fly if the dropdown does not offer it)
   - **Vendor**: `Beacon Auto Shop`
   - **Cost**: `150.00`
   - **Date**: `05/10/2026`
6. Save the service record.

**Rules:**
- Do not modify the vehicle itself (model, driver, license plate).
- Do not create a contract.
- Do not create another vehicle.
- Create exactly one service record linked to this vehicle.
