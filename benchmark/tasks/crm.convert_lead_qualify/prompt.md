Go to the **CRM Leads** view.

Find the lead named **Silver Maple Network Refresh** with email
**`ops+netops@silvermaple.example.com`**.

Update that lead as follows:

1. Open the lead.
2. Click **Convert to Opportunity**.
3. In the conversion dialog, keep the default conversion settings and click
   **Create Opportunity**.
4. After the lead is converted, move it to the **Qualified** stage.
5. Save the opportunity if needed.

**Rules:**
- Do not merge the lead with another lead or opportunity
- Do not create a quotation
- Do not edit any other lead or opportunity
- Do not change the contact email or create a new customer manually
