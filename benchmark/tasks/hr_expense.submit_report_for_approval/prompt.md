Go to the **Expenses** app and create a new expense for a recent business trip, then submit it for manager approval.

Create a new expense with these exact values:

- **Description**: `Hotel in Paris`
- **Category**: `Travel & Accommodation`
- **Total**: `250.00`
- **Expense Date**: `03/12/2026`

Save the expense, then click **Submit** (the status-bar button that moves the expense from Draft to Submitted).

**Rules:**
- Do not create more than one expense named `Hotel in Paris`.
- Do not change the employee on the expense.
- Do not approve, refuse, post, or reset the expense after submitting it.
- The expense must end in the **Submitted** state.
