Go to the **Purchase** app.

Find the draft RFQ with **Vendor Reference "BENCH-RFQ-DUP"** for vendor
**Meridian Supply Co**.

Duplicate this RFQ and reissue the copy to a different vendor:

1. Open the RFQ.
2. Click the **gear/cog** (Actions) menu in the control panel and choose
   **Duplicate**.
3. On the duplicated draft:
   - Change the **Vendor** to **Pinnacle Hardware**.
   - Set the **Vendor Reference** to **"PH-REISSUE-77"**.
   - Set **Order Deadline** to **05/28/2026**.
4. Click **Confirm Order** to convert the duplicate from RFQ into a
   confirmed purchase order.

**Rules:**
- Do NOT modify the original `BENCH-RFQ-DUP` RFQ. It must remain in draft
  for the original vendor with its lines untouched.
- Keep the two product lines on the duplicate exactly as copied — same
  products, quantities, and unit prices.
- Do NOT delete any RFQs and do NOT create one from scratch.
