website builder, with the editor enabled.

1. **Type**: `Selection` (renders as a single-choice multi-option field).
2. **Label**: `Inquiry Type`
3. **Options** (in this order):

example just above the Your Question textarea or as the last field before the
Submit button).

When you're done:

5. Click the **Save** button (top-right of the editor) and wait for the editor

- Change any existing field labels, types, or required flags.
