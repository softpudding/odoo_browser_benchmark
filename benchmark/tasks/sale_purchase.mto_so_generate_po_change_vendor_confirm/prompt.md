Confirm the MTO sale order, switch the generated RFQ to the alternate vendor, and confirm that purchase order.

1. Open the quotation with customer reference `MTO-VENDOR-SWITCH` for customer `Northwind MTO`.
2. Confirm the quotation.
3. On the confirmed sales order, click the `Purchase` smart button to open the generated RFQ.
4. Change the RFQ vendor from `Atlas Components` to `Beacon Components`.
5. Make sure the RFQ keeps product `Bench Relay Module`, quantity `3`, and unit price `62.00`.
6. Save the RFQ and click `Confirm Order`.

Rules:
- Do not change the customer, product, or ordered quantity.
- Do not create an extra quotation, RFQ, or purchase order.
- Do not cancel the sale order or the purchase order.
