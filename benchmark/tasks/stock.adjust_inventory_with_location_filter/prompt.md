Go to the **Inventory** app and open **Operations > Physical Inventory**.

There are stock entries for product **Servo Motor A1** in two different internal
locations. Filter the list so you work on location **WH/Stock** and set the
on-hand quantity for **Servo Motor A1** to **18**.

Leave the **WH/Quality** quantity unchanged.

After updating the target line, apply the inventory adjustment.

**Rules:**
- Only change the quantity in **WH/Stock**
- Do not edit the **WH/Quality** row
- Do not create new inventory lines
