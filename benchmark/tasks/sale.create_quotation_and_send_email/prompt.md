Open the Sales app. You need to create a brand new quotation from scratch and send it to the customer by email.

**Step 1 — Create the quotation:**

1. Click **New** to create a new quotation.
2. Set the customer to **Riverton Supply Co.**.
3. Add the following product lines:

| Product            | Qty | Unit Price |
|--------------------|-----|------------|
| Industrial Fan     | 6   | 185        |
| Air Filter Cartridge | 12  | 45        |

4. Set the **Expiration Date** to **2026-05-15**.
5. Save the quotation.

**Step 2 — Send by email:**

6. Click the **Send by Email** button on the quotation.
7. In the email compose wizard that appears, click **Send** to send the quotation to the customer.

After completing these steps, the quotation should be in **Quotation Sent** status.

**Rules:**
- The customer must be Riverton Supply Co.
- Product lines must match exactly (names, quantities, unit prices)
- Do not confirm the quotation — only send it by email
- The final status must be "Quotation Sent" (not Draft, not Sales Order)
