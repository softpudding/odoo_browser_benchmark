Open the published eCommerce product **`Bench SEO Widget`** and set its SEO metadata.

Steps:

1. From the **eCommerce Products** page (`/odoo/ecommerce-products`), open the product `Bench SEO Widget`. Since it is already published, clicking the card takes you to its front-end shop page inside the website preview.
2. While viewing the product's shop page, open the top-navbar **`Site`** menu → **`Optimize SEO`** (under the *This Page* section). This opens the **Optimize SEO** dialog.
3. In the dialog, set:
   - **Title**: `Buy Bench SEO Widget Online`
   - **Description**: `Premium widget for benchmarking with fast delivery.`
4. Click **Save** to close the dialog and persist the SEO fields.

**Rules:**
- Do not rename the product itself.
- Do not change the product URL, price, or any other field.
- Leave the keyword list and cover image untouched.
