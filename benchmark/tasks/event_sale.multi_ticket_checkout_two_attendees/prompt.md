# Sell two different event tickets in one order and fill two attendee details

You are in Odoo 19 logged in as **Administrator**. The events team has
set up the conference **Bench Summit 2026** (July 10, 2026) with two
paid ticket types:

- **Early Bird** — linked to service product **Bench Summit Early Bird
  Ticket** (list price 180.00)
- **VIP** — linked to service product **Bench Summit VIP Ticket**
  (list price 420.00)

The customer **Bench Summit Customer** has already been created in
Contacts.

You need to sell BOTH tickets (one of each) to **Bench Summit Customer**
in a **single confirmed sales order** and fill in a **different**
attendee's name and email on each of the two auto-generated event
registrations.

## Step 1 — Create one sales order with two ticket lines

1. Open the **Sales** app.
2. Click **New** to create a new quotation.
3. Set the **Customer** to `Bench Summit Customer`.
4. In the **Order Lines** tab, click **Add a product** and pick
   `Bench Summit Early Bird Ticket`.
   - If an **Configure an event** dialog opens, set:
     - **Event**: `Bench Summit 2026`
     - **Event Ticket**: `Early Bird`
   - Click **Ok** to close the configurator.
   - Make sure the line **Quantity** is `1`.
5. On a NEW order line, click **Add a product** again and pick
   `Bench Summit VIP Ticket`.
   - If an **Configure an event** dialog opens, set:
     - **Event**: `Bench Summit 2026`
     - **Event Ticket**: `VIP`
   - Click **Ok** to close the configurator.
   - Make sure the line **Quantity** is `1`.
6. Click **Confirm** to confirm the sales order. This creates two
   separate **event.registration** records — one for the Early Bird
   ticket and one for the VIP ticket.

## Step 2 — Fill attendee details on BOTH registrations

Use these exact attendee values:

| Ticket type | Attendee Name            | Email                              |
|-------------|--------------------------|------------------------------------|
| Early Bird  | `Alice Summit Attendee`  | `alice.summit@example.com`         |
| VIP         | `Bob Summit Attendee`    | `bob.summit@example.com`           |

Fill them by either of these two paths:

- If the **Event Registrations** attendee-details dialog pops up when
  you confirm the SO, it will have two rows — one per ticket. Fill the
  **Early Bird** row with Alice's name/email, fill the **VIP** row
  with Bob's name/email, and click **Create/Update registrations** to
  close the dialog.

- Otherwise, open the **Events** app, open **Bench Summit 2026**,
  click the **Attendees** stat button, and edit EACH of the two
  auto-generated registrations individually:
  - On the **Early Bird** registration, set **Attendee Name** to
    `Alice Summit Attendee` and **Email** to `alice.summit@example.com`.
  - On the **VIP** registration, set **Attendee Name** to
    `Bob Summit Attendee` and **Email** to `bob.summit@example.com`.

  Save each registration.

## Final state

- **Exactly one sale.order** for **Bench Summit Customer** in
  **Sales Order** state (`sale`), containing exactly two order lines:
  one for `Bench Summit Early Bird Ticket` (qty 1, Early Bird ticket on
  Bench Summit 2026) and one for `Bench Summit VIP Ticket` (qty 1, VIP
  ticket on Bench Summit 2026). The order `amount_total` must equal
  **600.00**.
- **Exactly two event.registration** records on **Bench Summit 2026**:
  - Early Bird ticket registration with **Attendee Name**
    `Alice Summit Attendee` and **Email** `alice.summit@example.com`.
  - VIP ticket registration with **Attendee Name**
    `Bob Summit Attendee` and **Email** `bob.summit@example.com`.
  - Both registrations are in **Registered** (`open`) state.

## Rules

- Use the seeded ticket products and ticket types exactly. Do NOT
  create a new product and do NOT add a new ticket type on the event.
- Create **exactly one** sales order and **exactly two**
  registrations (one per ticket generated by confirming the order).
- Do **NOT** swap the two attendees: `Alice Summit Attendee` is the
  **Early Bird** attendee, `Bob Summit Attendee` is the **VIP**
  attendee. Email casing must match exactly.
- Do **NOT** cancel, check-in, or set-done the registrations; they must
  stay in the **Registered** (`open`) state.
- Do **NOT** modify the event name, dates, or the ticket definitions.
