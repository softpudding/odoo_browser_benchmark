Go to the **CRM** app.

Find the lead **`Summit Ridge Support Rollout`**.

Update it as follows:

1. Click **Convert to Opportunity**.
2. In the conversion dialog, choose **Link to an existing customer** and select **`Summit Ridge Manufacturing`**.
3. Set the stage to **Qualified**.
4. Save the opportunity if needed.

**Rules:**
- Do not create a new customer
- Do not create a quotation
- Do not modify any other lead or opportunity
- Do not change the salesperson
