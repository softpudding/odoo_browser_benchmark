Go to the **Sales** app.

Find the draft quotation with Customer Reference **"BENCH-DUP-01"** for
customer **Azure Interior**.

Duplicate this quotation and reassign the copy to a different customer:

1. Open the quotation.
2. Click the **gear/cog** (Actions) menu in the control panel and choose
   **Duplicate** to create an exact copy.
3. The new draft quotation opens automatically. On that copy:
   - Change the **Customer** field to **Summit Ventures**.
   - Set the **Customer Reference** to **"SV-DUP-2026"**.
4. Click **Save** (or the cloud/save icon in the control panel). Leave the
   duplicate in **Draft** — do NOT click Confirm.

**Rules:**
- Do NOT modify the original `BENCH-DUP-01` quotation in any way.
- Keep both order lines on the duplicate exactly as they were copied —
  same products, quantities, and unit prices.
- Do NOT delete the duplicate, and do NOT confirm it.
- Do NOT create an entirely new quotation from scratch.
