Go to the **Employees** app.

By default the Employees list hides archived records. You need to show
them to find the target:

1. Open the search filter dropdown (the **Filters** button) and tick
   **Archived**.
2. In the filtered list, open the employee **"Avery Collins"**.
3. Open the **gear/cog (Actions)** menu in the control panel and click
   **Unarchive**.
4. After the form re-opens, set the **Department** field to **"Engineering"**.
5. Click **Save**.

**Rules:**
- Do NOT create a new employee — unarchive the existing one.
- Do NOT change the employee's name, job title, work email, or manager.
- Do NOT touch any other employee.
