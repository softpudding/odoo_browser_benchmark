Open the seeded website page **`Bench Product Landing`** (URL `/bench-product`) and set its SEO metadata.

Steps:

1. From the **Website** app (`/odoo/website`), navigate to the page `/bench-product` so the page is shown inside the website preview. You can either pick it from **Site > Pages** or type `/bench-product` into the address bar of the preview.
2. While viewing the page, open the top-navbar **`Site`** menu. Under the *This Page* section, click **`Optimize SEO`**. This opens the **Optimize SEO** dialog.
3. In the dialog, set:
   - **Title** (meta title): `Bench Product - Premium Quality | Bench Co`
   - **Description** (meta description): `Discover our flagship Bench Product with premium materials and lifetime warranty. Order today for free shipping.`
   - **Keywords**: add the following three keywords one at a time (press Enter after each, or click the add button):
     - `bench product`
     - `premium bench`
     - `quality furniture`
4. Click **Save** to close the dialog and persist the SEO fields.

**Rules:**
- Do not rename the page or change its URL.
- Do not unpublish the page.
- Do not modify any other content on the page.
- Keep the cover image untouched.
