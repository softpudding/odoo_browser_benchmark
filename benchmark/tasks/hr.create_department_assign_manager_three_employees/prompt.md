# Set Up the Bench Engineering Department

You need to spin up a new department and move three engineers into it.

## Step 1 - Create the department

1. Go to the **Employees** app, then open the **Departments** view (left sidebar **Departments** menu).
2. Click **New** to create a new department.
3. Set the department **Name** to `Bench Engineering`.
4. Set the **Manager** to `Mike Manager`.
5. **Save** the department record.

## Step 2 - Reassign the three engineers

Open the **Employees** app's main employee list and, for each of the
following employees in turn, open their form, change the **Department**
field to `Bench Engineering`, and **Save**:

1. `Eng One`
2. `Eng Two`
3. `Eng Three`

## Rules

- The department name must be exactly `Bench Engineering`.
- The department manager must be `Mike Manager`.
- All three engineers (`Eng One`, `Eng Two`, `Eng Three`) must end up in
  the `Bench Engineering` department.
- Do not change any other fields on the engineers (job title, work
  email, phone, etc.).
- Do not archive or delete any employees or departments.
- Do not modify Mike Manager's own department.
