Upvote an existing forum question on the Bench Forum and mark the existing answer as the accepted answer.

1. Open the public forum at `/forum`. Click the **Bench Forum** tile to enter it.
2. Click the question titled `How do I install?` (posted by user **Bench Bot**) to open the question page. The question already has exactly one existing answer by Bench Bot.
3. On the question row (top of the page, the question content block on the left edge), click the **upvote** arrow (the small ↑ caret-up icon above the vote count) so the question's vote count goes from 0 to 1. The arrow turns green once your upvote is recorded. Do NOT click the downvote caret-down arrow below the count.
4. Scroll to Bench Bot's existing answer (content starts with `Run the install pipeline ...`). In the vote column on the LEFT of that answer (not the question), click the circle-check **Accept** icon so the answer is marked as the best / correct answer. A green filled checkmark indicates it is accepted.

Rules:
- Do NOT post a new answer, a comment, or any new content.
- Do NOT edit, delete, close, or re-title the seeded question or answer.
- Do NOT upvote or downvote the ANSWER — only the QUESTION should be upvoted.
- Do NOT click the downvote caret on the question.
- After the run, the question must have exactly one upvote from admin (vote_count=1) and the one seeded answer must be accepted (green check). No new forum posts should exist.
