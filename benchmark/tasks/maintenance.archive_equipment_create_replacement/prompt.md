# Retire an Old Belt and Register Its Replacement

You will retire an old equipment record and create its replacement in the
**Maintenance** app.

## Step 1 — Archive the old equipment

1. Open the **Maintenance** app.
2. From the top menu, open **Equipments**.
3. Open the existing equipment named `Old Belt OB-99` (it is in the
   `Warehouse Gear` category).
4. Open the **Actions** menu (the gear / cog button in the control panel,
   next to the breadcrumbs) and click **Archive**.
5. Confirm any dialog that appears.

The record must be **archived**, not deleted. Use the *Archive* action — do
not click *Delete*.

## Step 2 — Create the replacement equipment

1. Go back to the **Equipments** list (you can use the breadcrumb).
2. Click **New** to create a new equipment record.
3. Set the equipment **Name** to `New Belt NB-100`.
4. Set the **Equipment Category** to `Warehouse Gear` (pick the existing
   category).
5. Open the **Product Information** tab and set the **Serial Number** to
   `NB-100-SN-2026`.
6. Save the record.

## Rules

- Use the exact equipment names, category, and serial number shown above.
- The `Warehouse Gear` category already exists — select it, do not create a
  new one.
- The old equipment `Old Belt OB-99` must be **archived** (Actions → Archive),
  not deleted. Do not unarchive it.
- The new equipment `New Belt NB-100` must be saved and remain **active**
  (do not archive it).
- Do not modify or delete any other existing equipment records.
