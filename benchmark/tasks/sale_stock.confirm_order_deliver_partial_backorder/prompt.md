Navigate to the **Sales** app and open the **Quotations** list.

Find the draft quotation with customer reference **"BENCH-SO-BACK"** for
customer **Westfield Manufacturing**.

1. Open the quotation and click **Confirm** to convert it into a Sales Order.
2. Click the **Delivery** smart button to navigate to the delivery order.
3. On the delivery order, set the **Done** quantities for each line as follows
   (this is a partial shipment):
   - **Precision Bearing Kit**: Done = **15** (out of 25 ordered)
   - **Stainless Steel Rod 2m**: Done = **40** (out of 40 ordered — full qty)
   - **Titanium Bolt Set M12**: Done = **6** (out of 20 ordered)
4. Click **Validate**.
5. In the backorder dialog that appears, click **Create Backorder**
   (do NOT click "No Backorder").

**Rules:**
- You MUST set the exact Done quantities listed above.
- You MUST choose **Create Backorder** in the dialog — do NOT choose
  "No Backorder".
- Do NOT modify any other quotation or sales order.
- Do NOT change the products, customer, or prices on the order.
