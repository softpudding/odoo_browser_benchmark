Go to the **Sales** app.

Find the confirmed sales order for customer **Northwind Studio** with
Customer Reference **`MED-INV-SO-01`**.

Create the invoice for that order and update it as follows:

1. Open the sales order and click **Create Invoice**.
2. If a dialog appears, keep the default invoice option and click
   **Create Draft**.
3. On the draft customer invoice, set **Invoice Date** to **05/12/2026**
   (May 12, 2026).
4. Set **Payment Terms** to **30 Net Days**.
5. Click **Confirm** to post the invoice.

**Rules:**
- Do not modify any other sales order or invoice
- Do not change the product list, quantities, or unit prices
- Do not register a payment
- Do not create more than one invoice for the target order
