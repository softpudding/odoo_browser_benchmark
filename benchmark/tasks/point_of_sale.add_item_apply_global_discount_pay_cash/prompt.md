Go to the **Point of Sale** app and open the register **`Bench Test Store`**.

1. Add **`Bench POS Widget`** to the cart and set the quantity to **2** (€20 each, so the lines should subtotal to **€40.00**).
2. Apply a **15% global discount** to the order using the on-screen **`Discount`** control button (the one with the tag icon, sometimes labelled `Disc`). When the percentage popup opens, enter **`15`** and confirm. The order total must drop to **€34.00**.
3. Click **Payment**, choose **`Bench Cash`** as the payment method, type the exact tendered amount **`34`** on the keypad, then click **Validate** so the receipt screen appears.

## Rules

- Do not add any other product
- Do not change the unit price of `Bench POS Widget`
- Do not invoice the order
- Do not close the POS session after the sale
- Use the global Discount control button — do **not** apply a per-line discount
