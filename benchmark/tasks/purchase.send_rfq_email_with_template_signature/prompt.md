Go to the **Purchase** app.

Find the draft Request for Quotation for vendor **Beacon Electrical Ltd.** with
Vendor Reference **`R-BENCH-EMAIL-001`** and open it.

Send it to the vendor by email. Before sending you must switch the email
template and then append a short instruction at the bottom of the message:

1. Click the **Send RFQ** button in the form header.
2. A **Compose Email** dialog opens. It is pre-loaded with the
   **Purchase: Request For Quotation** template.
3. **Change the email template** to **Purchase: Purchase Order** using
   the template selector at the bottom of the compose dialog. After you
   switch the template the message body is replaced with the new
   template's content (it mentions "Here is in attachment a purchase
   order").
4. Once the new template body is loaded, **append** the following sentence
   at the end of the message body (on a new line, AFTER the template
   signature/closing):

   `Please confirm by Friday.`

   The final body must contain the **Purchase: Purchase Order** template
   text PLUS your appended sentence. Do not delete any of the template
   text.
5. Click the **Send** button at the bottom of the dialog.

After sending, the RFQ's status bar should change from **RFQ** to
**RFQ Sent**.

**Rules:**
- You MUST switch the template to **Purchase: Purchase Order** before
  appending and sending. Do not send with the default **Purchase: Request
  For Quotation** template.
- You MUST append the sentence AFTER switching the template — switching
  the template after typing will wipe your appended text.
- Do not change the Subject line.
- Do not change the vendor, product list, quantities, or unit prices.
- Do not confirm the order (do not click Confirm Order).
- Do not cancel the RFQ.
- Do not touch any other RFQ or purchase order.
