Go to the **Purchase** app.

Find the confirmed purchase order with **Vendor Reference "BENCH-BILL-PO"**
for vendor **Meridian Supply Co**.

1. Open the purchase order.
2. Click the **Create Bill** button (in the status bar at the top of the
   form). This opens a draft vendor bill pre-filled from the PO.
3. On the draft vendor bill:
   - Set the **Bill Date** to **May 3, 2026**.
   - Set the **Bill Reference** to **"MSC-BILL-9921"**.
4. Click **Confirm** to confirm (post) the vendor bill.

**Rules:**
- Do NOT change the product lines, quantities, or unit prices on the
  generated bill.
- Do NOT go back and edit the PO itself.
- Do NOT register a payment — Confirm is the final step.
